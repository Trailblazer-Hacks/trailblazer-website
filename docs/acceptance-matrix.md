## Concrete improvement evidence

- Original supplied image was 6,436,153 bytes. The served derivative is 82,608 bytes, a **98.7% reduction**, and loads successfully in the browser. The large original is not in exported public assets.
- Initial axe runs failed section-label contrast (ratios 4.37 and 4.39 against a 4.5 requirement). Increasing label opacity removed those reported violations on desktop and mobile. Subsequent axe runs passed.
- Original website repo was empty. The current export is exercised through actual header navigation, all seven keyboard FAQ toggles, native no-JavaScript disclosure, venue-link query, and confirmed-fact rendering. These are observed usable outputs, not “files exist” proxies.
- Exact-role validation initially failed three vault titles. After correction, all nine role assertions and the full source/pitch/link validator pass.
- Coverage grew from one FAQ interaction and broad section checks to every FAQ, sticky CTA after scrolling, every confirmed logistics field, real artwork loading, metadata/favicon, actual request origins, and normal-preference mobile motion behavior.

Remaining boundaries are explicit: no public deployment, live registration, full video review, native Obsidian opening, real-device/screen-reader audit, or human aesthetic approval. None is silently counted as passed.
